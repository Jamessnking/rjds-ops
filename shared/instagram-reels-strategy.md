---

## Core Principles

1. **Sends per reach is the #1 cold-reach signal.** A DM share counts ~3-5x more than a like for pushing to non-followers. Design every Reel and CTA to earn a share.
2. **Watch time is the biggest single signal.** First 3 seconds is the inflection point — drop-off before 3 seconds caps the seed test.
3. **Test cold before committing.** Use Trial Reels first (if 1K+ followers). A flop never touches your follower feed metrics.
4. **Originality is enforced, not encouraged.** No TikTok/CapCut watermarks. Shoot/edit your own footage. Keep rolling 30-day window majority-original.
5. **Captions + on-screen text = SEO. Hashtags are NOT.** Mosseri confirmed: hashtags don't improve reach. Put keywords in the caption and on screen. Max 3-5 hashtags.
6. **Reels is a discovery surface, not a follower surface.** Plan every Reel for someone who has never heard of you.

---

## The Perfect Reel — Step by Step

1. **Hook (first 3 seconds):** Concrete, specific, no "hey guys". On-screen text for sound-off viewers. Must preview a shareable payoff.
2. **Structure for sends, not likes.** Build so the payoff is something one specific person needs to see. CTA = "send this to the friend who [specific situation]" — NOT "follow for more".
3. **Originality + audio check.** No watermarks, original footage, audio fits account type (Creator vs Business access differs).
4. **Caption.** Keyword in first line, send-prompt CTA, 3-5 hashtags max.
5. **After publish: read Insights.** Key metrics: skip rate, share rate, followers from this post. Viral views without follows = Reel didn't make the topic legible.

---

## Reel Script Structure (15-30 seconds)

- **0-3s:** Hook — name the problem or payoff. Text on screen. No intro.
- **3-15s:** Deliver the value fast. Each beat earns the next second.
- **15-25s:** Payoff — the thing they'll want to share.
- **25-30s:** CTA — "Send this to [specific person]" or "Save this."

---

## What Kills Reach

- Reposts / watermarked content (cut from cold distribution)
- "Hey guys" intro (first 3 seconds dead)
- "Follow for more" CTA (suppresses sends)
- Long intro before the hook
- Hashtag stuffing
- Bad audio (copyrighted for Business accounts)

---

## Trial Reels — Use This

If goal = cold reach and account has 1K+ followers:
- Post as Trial Reel first
- Read at 24 hours: if send rate is good, publish to everyone
- If it flops, it never touched your main feed

---

## Diagnostics — Why a Reel Flopped

Check in order:
1. Originality flag? (repurposed content)
2. 3-second drop-off? (weak hook)
3. Send signal low? (no send-prompt CTA)
4. Audio stripped? (music licensing issue)
5. Caption SEO? (keyword not in first line)

---

## Key Metrics to Track

- **Skip rate** — hook strength
- **Share rate** — send signal (most important for cold reach)
- **Retention drop-off** — where people leave
- **Followers from this post** — topic legibility score
- **Watch time** — overall engagement quality
